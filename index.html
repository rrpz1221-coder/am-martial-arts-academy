About the academy

A working fight house in Kalyan.

Founded in a borrowed school hall in 2018. Now Kalyan's quiet authority on combat sport for adults and kids.

The academy was started in 2018 by a then-amateur fighter who needed somewhere honest to train. Eight years on, the room hasn't gotten softer. It has gotten better. Round timers tick. Pads pop. Nothing here is for the camera.

Coach Arshiyan Memon still leads every session personally. The roster ranges from college students after class learning to defend themselves, to working professionals shaking off ten years of office-chair atrophy, to junior amateurs prepping for their first state-level card.

What ties them together is the same thing that ties any real fight house together: a refusal to coast. The room watches you. The room asks for the next round. The room is the coach when the coach is across the mat.

The cycle

Twelve weeks,
four chapters.

Every program runs on the same structured arc. Intake every quarter. What changes is the work in the room, not the math around it.
Wk / 01

Intake.

Movement screen, conditioning baseline, video reference frame on a plain backdrop. Coach reads your individual delta: the gap between where you walk in and where the room takes you.

You leave with A baseline. A plan.
Wk / 04

Foundations.

Stance, footwork, the four primary strikes, breakfalls, the basic clinch. Drilled until they survive fatigue. First coach check-in at week-end. A five-minute conversation, not a sales pitch.

You leave with Honest mechanics.
Wk / 08

Live work.

Controlled sparring under fatigue. Combinations against a thinking partner. Round timer running. The room begins to test you instead of teach you. This is the chapter most students remember.

You leave with Composure under pressure.
Wk / 12

The card.

A documented full-cycle session. Same camera, same light, same stance as Wk 01. Second coach check-in at week ten. The cycle closes, the next opens. Most students re-enrol inside seven days.

You leave with A fighter's reference frame.

Four cycles a year. Same arc. Same coach. Same Reti Bunder address.
